in `licenses/`, from Espressif esp-dev-kits commit
`5c06bf1835329d6525f316475f7218aab6acfe79`. Their Git blob IDs are respectively
`e1ad66d93e2a2113bde9839366fd6a6077a9ca62` and
`795087c264ddd3059b2ab3945a74d11930e7c10e`.

and asset checksums are retained exactly as tested on the board. Upstream
artwork retains its original license scope. Backend notices and source
headers remain alongside their respective files.
